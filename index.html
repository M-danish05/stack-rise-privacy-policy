Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Stack Rise ("the App"). Your privacy matters, and this policy is designed to be transparent about data practices.

Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game data, including high scores and sound preferences, is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Data Storage

The App uses local device storage to save your high score and sound settings. This data remains entirely on your device and is not accessible to anyone else.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising frameworks, or tracking technologies.

Internet Access

The App functions entirely offline and does not require or use an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy
